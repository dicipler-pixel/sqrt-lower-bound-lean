# What is not proved here

Lean proves exactly the statements written, under exactly the hypotheses written.

* Only the **lower** square-root bound is formalized. The matching upper bound, the saturation
  statement and the `+ log t` correction of the paper are not.
* The statements concern the concrete rectangular finite-field circuit with a nontrivial additive
  character in odd characteristic. The Rényi-entropy results (linear growth for `0 ≤ α < 1`,
  logarithmic for `α > 1`), integrability of the gate and the braid relation are the paper's and
  are not formalized here.
* No physical interpretation is formalized.

Formalization is evidence for the mathematics, not for the physical interpretation.
